# Goals of Self-Replication
The goals of Jonnies work is summarized on pg 92 with 5 main questions:

1. Logical universiality
1. Constructability
1. Constructable universality
1. Self-production
1. Evolution

He attributes 1. to Turing has having been solved and solves 2-4 in his work [1]. He mentions that 5 is unknown and needs further work to solve and is outside his work.
