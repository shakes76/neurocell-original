# Notes on the Brain
Johnnie wrote his notes on the brain in his work "The Computer and the Brain".

## Neurons
Johnnie believed the digital nature of the neuron defining them as a non-linear mechanism whereby it sent a pulse (representing one) and in an absence of a pulse (representing zero) (pg. 43).

## Connectivity of Neurons
In his section on Complicated Simulation Criteria (pg. 54), where he envision that neuron not only could fire based on the number of connections (and subsequent connections to firing neurons) but to the spatial relationships of the neurons and their synapses as well "by their coverage of certain regions on that neuron".

## Simultaneity
Johnnie even considers how the propagation of signals arrive and that there is a processing delay to allow signals to arrive called the propagation time call summation time (pg. 56).

## Thoughts
The definition of neurons and his later work on replicating machines show that state of the neuron is an important aspect in deciding a neuron role in the computation.

The connectivity that Johnnie conjectured is indeed the learning of the spatial filters within the neurocell framework. These filters are learnt that connect various neurons not by merely immediate neighbours, but spatially across multiple 'layers'.

The summation time and propagation of signals also shows up in neurocell as the computation of each forward step of the network.
